Technical: The Fresnel Integrals

x(s) = ∫₀ˢ cos(t²) dt   ·   y(s) = ∫₀ˢ sin(t²) dt

The Fresnel integrals C(s) and S(s) oscillate with decreasing amplitude, converging to ½. These are the x and y coordinates that trace the spiral.

Curvature: κ = 2s

Linear curvature growth — the signature property

Unlike a circle (constant curvature) or a logarithmic spiral (exponential), the Euler spiral's curvature grows linearly. Each step forward adds the same increment of turning — the geometry of steady, compounding progress.

Connections

Mathematical Thread

The Euler spiral is ∫eit²dt — the integrand is a conformal map. Escher's Print Gallery is built from ez mapping strips to annuli. The Droste effect is the periodicity of log(z). All conformal maps on C, all preserving local shape while transforming global structure. Pretext's obstacle-routing geometry connects these ideas to programmatic typography.

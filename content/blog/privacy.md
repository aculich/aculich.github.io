# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 1, 2026

This site ([aculich.github.io](https://aculich.github.io), “Phase Shift”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected when you visit, how it is used, and your choices.

## What we collect

### Information you provide

This is a static blog. There are no accounts, comments, or contact forms on the site itself. If you email me directly, that correspondence is handled separately.

### Information collected automatically (with your consent)

If you **accept** analytics cookies via the cookie banner, we use:

- **Google Analytics 4** — pages visited, approximate geography, device/browser type, referral source, time on page. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** — cookieless, aggregate pageview metrics (also loaded only after you accept analytics, for consistency). [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/)

If you **decline** cookies, or if your browser sends a **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** opt-out signal, analytics scripts do not run.

We **do not sell or share** personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Cookies

| Type | Purpose | Required? |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Essential | Site functionality (none beyond basic delivery today) | — |
| Analytics | GA4 first-party cookies; Cloudflare beacon (no cookies) | Opt-in only |

You can change your choice anytime by clearing site data and revisiting, or use browser controls to block cookies.

## Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/) as a valid opt-out of analytics tracking. When GPC is enabled, we do not show the cookie banner and do not load analytics.

This site publishes [`/.well-known/gpc.json`](https://aculich.github.io/.well-known/gpc.json) indicating GPC support.

## Your rights

### California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to opt out of sale/sharing (we do not sell data), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Contact me below to make a request.

### European residents (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object, and withdraw consent. Analytics runs only after opt-in consent.

## Data retention

Analytics providers retain data according to their policies (typically 2–14 months for GA4). I use aggregate reports only.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
